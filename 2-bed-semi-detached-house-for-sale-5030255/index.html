Description

MURPHY MULLAN are delighted to present this fantastic two bedroomed house to the market. Set in a small development off Blackhorse Avenue. This property is sure to appeal to firs time buyers and downsizers alike.

The location is simply superb with the Phoenix Park on its door step providing both country and city living in the heart of Dublin with stunning walks and scenery. There are excellent local schools and a host of shops and necessary amenities. With an excellent public transport system to and from Dublin City Centre with both the N3 and M50 close by providing easy access to most route ways. Broombridge green luas line is only 15 minutes’ walk making many locations easily accessible.

Well-proportioned accommodation downstairs includes hallway, living room, kitchen and conservatory. Upstairs two double bedrooms and main bathroom.

Viewing is highly recommended!
